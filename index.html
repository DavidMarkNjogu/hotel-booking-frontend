Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in time is 3:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability.

Do you offer airport shuttle service?

Yes, we provide complimentary airport shuttle service for all our guests. Please inform us of your arrival time at least 24 hours in advance.

Is parking available?

Yes, we offer free parking for all guests. We have both covered and open parking spaces available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, we are pet-friendly! Pet-friendly rooms are available for an additional fee of $50 per night. Please inform us in advance when booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled check-in date. Cancellations made within 48 hours will be charged one night's stay.
